## ✅ What is expected

Discuss the work. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Disagree about substance as strongly as you like, and leave the person out of it. If you are correcting something, say what is wrong and what would be right.

## 🚫 What is not accepted

Harassment, personal abuse, discriminatory remarks, sustained disruption, or publishing anybody's private information. None of it, in issues, pull requests, commit messages or anywhere else connected to this repository.

## 🔒 The rule that is specific to this project

**Never post real data.** No customer records, no production schemas, no internal system names, no screenshots of live tables, no client identities. This applies whether the data is yours or somebody else's.

A comment containing real data is deleted rather than edited, because editing does not remove content from a public repository's history. That is not a judgement on the person posting it, it is the only way to remove it.

## 🌐 Scope

This applies anywhere connected to this repository, and to anyone taking part, including the maintainer.

## 📮 Reporting

NeumannTechTips@pm.me. Reports are handled privately and in confidence.

Depending on what has happened, the response ranges from a private word to deleting content, closing a thread, or blocking an account. The maintainer decides, and will say what was decided and why.
